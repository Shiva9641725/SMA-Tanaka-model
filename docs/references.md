## 📚 References

## 1. Tanaka (1986)

**Tanaka, K. (1986).**  
"A Thermomechanical Sketch of Shape Memory Effect: One-Dimensional Tensile Behavior."  
*Res Mechanica, 18(3), 251–263.*  
https://sid.ir/paper/586962/en  

This paper introduced the smooth hyperbolic-tangent transition used for modeling SMA phase transformation.  
It provides the mathematical foundation for the transformation law used in this repository.

---

## 2. Ben Hatira & Saidane (2012)

**Ben Hatira, F., & Saidane, K. (2012).**  
"A Thermo-Mechanical Behavior Simulation of a NiTi Staple Used for the Correction of Idiopathic Scoliosis."  
*Journal of Biomaterials and Nanobiotechnology, 3, 61–69.*  
doi:10.4236/jbnb.2012.31008  

This study provides representative NiTi parameters (transformation temperatures, elastic modulus ≈70 GPa, transformation strain 3–6%).  
It supports the parameter ranges used in this model.

---

## 3. Costanza, Tata, & Danaee (2025)

**Costanza, G., Tata, M. E., & Danaee Barforooshi, S. (2025).**  
"Phenomenological Modeling of Shape Memory Alloys: A Review of Macroscopic Approaches."  
*Micromachines, 16(11), 1300.*  
https://doi.org/10.3390/mi16111300  

This review summarizes major phenomenological SMA models (Tanaka, Brinson, Liang–Rogers, Lagoudas).  
It validates the modeling approach used in this repository.
