## 📚 References

**Tanaka, K. (1986).**  
*A Thermomechanical Sketch of Shape Memory Effect: One-Dimensional Tensile Behavior.*  
Res Mechanica, 18(3), 251–263.  
https://sid.ir/paper/586962/en  
This foundational paper introduced the hyperbolic-tangent (tanh) representation of
smooth phase transformation in SMAs, forming the mathematical basis for the transition
law used in this project.

**Ben Hatira, F., & Saidane, K. (2012).**  
*A Thermo-Mechanical Behavior Simulation of a NiTi Staple Used for the Correction of Idiopathic Scoliosis.*  
Journal of Biomaterials and Nanobiotechnology, 3, 61–69.  
doi:10.4236/jbnb.2012.31008  
This study provides representative NiTi transformation temperatures, elastic moduli,
and transformation strain values used in biomedical SMA applications, supporting the
parameter ranges selected in this model.

**Costanza, G., Tata, M. E., & Danaee Barforooshi, S. (2025).**  
*Phenomenological Modeling of Shape Memory Alloys: A Review of Macroscopic Approaches.*  
Micromachines, 16(11), 1300.  
https://doi.org/10.3390/mi16111300  
This review summarizes classical and modern phenomenological SMA models—including
Tanaka, Brinson, Liang-Rogers, and Boyd-Lagoudas formulations—providing broad context
and validation for the smooth macroscopic transition laws implemented in this repository.
